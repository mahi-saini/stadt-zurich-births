# Analysis 2: Births By Child Sex Per Year 

[← Back to main README](../README.md)

## What this shows
Number of births per year, split by child sex (male vs. female), from 1993 through 2025 

## Method
Grouped the cleaned dataset by `year` and `child_sex`, summing the `births` column for each

## Visualization

![Total births per year](../visualizations/analysis-2.png)

## Observations
- Male and female birth counts track each other closely across the full 33-year period 
- Both follow the same overall shape as total births: flat through the 1990s, rising sharply after 2005, peaking in the early 2020s, then pulling back 
- No sustained gap between the two, the sex split stays roughly balanced year to year rather than trending toward one side over time
